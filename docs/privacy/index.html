Privacy policy.

send n*des collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no server that your data passes through. Last updated 23 October 2026.

The short version

The apps send your files to a server you run and nowhere else. The developer never receives your files, your server address, your passphrase or any usage data.

What the iPhone app handles

  • Files, photos and links you share to it. They are encrypted on your phone with your vault's public inbox key, queued, and uploaded to the server address you paired with. They are not sent anywhere else.
  • Pairing details. The server address, a device name you choose, and an upload-only token. These are stored on the phone, in the iOS Keychain and a storage area shared only between the app and its share extension. They are not sent to the developer.
  • Camera. Used only to scan the pairing QR code from your Mac. Nothing is recorded or stored.
  • Network. The app connects only to the server you paired with, over HTTPS.

The phone holds an upload-only key. It cannot read, list or delete anything on your server or in your vault.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we never have any.

Your server and your files

The inbox server (ndesd) and the vault are yours. Whoever hosts them, including a cloud storage provider you choose, sees only ciphertext. How long files wait on your server, who can reach it and how it is backed up are up to you. Apple's own services (iCloud backup, the App Store, TestFlight) have their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Everything the app stores is on your device. Deleting the app removes it. Removing the device on your server's admin page revokes its token. Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version and date will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/klppl/sendndes/issues.
